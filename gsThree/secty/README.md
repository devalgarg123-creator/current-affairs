1) Digital payments and cyber fraud
A growing concern is that India's rapid expansion of digital payments is accompanied by increasing exposure to cyber fraud.
The issue involves:
- UPI fraud
- Phishing
- Social engineering
- Fake customer-care numbers
- Malware
- Identity theft
- Account takeover

Why important?
India's digital public infrastructure has increased Convenience + financial inclusion but also creates Large digital attack surface

UPSC framework
Digitalisation
→ greater financial inclusion
→ lower transaction costs
BUT
→ cyber vulnerabilities
→ fraud
→ loss of consumer trust

Way forward
- Stronger bank response mechanisms
- Faster fraud reporting
- Better customer awareness
- AI-based fraud detection
- Secure authentication
- Data protection
- Cross-border cyber cooperation
- Greater accountability across payment ecosystem

Mains question
"Trust is the most important infrastructure for a digital economy." Discuss in the context of cyber fraud in digital payments.
