1. Governance: Artificial Intelligence in Traffic Management

Urbanisation and increasing vehicle ownership have placed considerable pressure on India's urban transport infrastructure. Intelligent Traffic Management Systems (ITMS) use technologies such as artificial intelligence, sensors, cameras and real-time data analytics to improve traffic management.

C. Significance for India
Efficient urban mobility:Real-time monitoring can help authorities identify congestion and modify traffic signals according to traffic volume.

Road safety: Automated detection of traffic violations can support enforcement of traffic regulations.

Emergency response: Traffic monitoring and signal coordination can help emergency vehicles navigate congested roads.

Data-driven governance: Traffic data can help urban authorities identify congestion hotspots and plan infrastructure.

D. Challenges
High installation and maintenance costs.
Poor integration between different traffic management systems.
Privacy concerns arising from continuous surveillance.
Inadequate digital infrastructure in smaller cities.
Potential errors in AI-based detection.
