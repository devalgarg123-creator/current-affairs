1) Drinking Water in Tribal Areas

Why is it in the news?
The Department of Drinking Water and Sanitation held a follow-up convergence meeting to accelerate drinking water coverage in tribal areas under PM-JANMAN and DA-JGUA.

The meeting reviewed progress and called on states and Union Territories to identify habitations that remain without adequate drinking water access.

Background: Access to safe drinking water is an essential component of public health and human development.

Tribal habitations may face particular difficulties because of:
Remote geographical locations.
Dispersed settlements.
Inadequate water infrastructure.
Seasonal water scarcity.
Limited access to maintenance services.

Relevant government initiatives

Jal Jeevan Mission aims to provide functional household tap connections in rural areas, It also emphasises water quality, source sustainability and community participation.

Significance
Health: Safe drinking water can reduce the risk of waterborne diseases.
Education: Better access to water can reduce the time spent collecting water, particularly for women and children.
Gender equality: Improved household water access can reduce the burden of unpaid water collection work.
Tribal development: Water infrastructure can improve living standards in remote tribal communities.

Challenges
Difficult terrain and remote settlements.
Seasonal variation in water availability.
Groundwater contamination in some regions.
Inadequate maintenance of water infrastructure.
Coordination difficulties between departments.

Way forward
Prioritise habitations with the greatest infrastructure gaps.
Strengthen water quality testing.
Promote community participation in maintenance.
Ensure convergence between tribal development and water supply programmes.
Develop locally appropriate water supply systems.

2) 57th GST Council Meeting — GST 2.0 reforms
The 57th GST Council meeting is being held on 8 October 2026 under the chairpersonship of the Union Finance Minister.
The discussion is focused on the next phase of GST reforms, particularly:
- simplification of GST registration
- easier returns and refunds
- improving Input Tax Credit (ITC)
- faster dispute resolution
- reducing compliance burden
- reforms affecting e-commerce suppliers
- distinction between deliberate tax evasion and genuine/non-fraudulent defaults. Mid-Day
Static linkage
Article 279A → GST Council
GST Council is a constitutional body under Article 279A.
Composition:
- Union Finance Minister — Chairperson
- Union Minister of State in charge of Revenue/Finance
- State Finance/Taxation Ministers
The larger issue is the balance between:
Ease of doing business ↔ tax enforcement ↔ cooperative federalism
A particularly important issue is the debate around arrest powers under GST. Under the CGST framework, Section 69 deals with arrest in specified circumstances linked to offences under Section 132. Mid-Day
Mains line:
GST's next phase should move from merely harmonising indirect taxation towards improving tax certainty, compliance efficiency and cooperative federalism.
