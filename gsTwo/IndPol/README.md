1. Gujarat Uniform Civil Code Bill gets Presidential assent

Why in news?
The Gujarat Uniform Civil Code (UCC) Bill, 2026 has received the President's assent. The President's official record lists the assent date as 17 September 2026, while the Bill was published in Gujarat's extraordinary gazette on October 1.

What does the Gujarat UCC cover?

UCC can address inequalities relating to :
Marriage
Divorce
Succession/inheritance
Adoption
Live-in relationships

It largely follows the model of Uttarakhand's UCC.

Important: The law excludes Scheduled Tribes and certain groups whose customary rights are protected under Part XXI of the Constitution.

Relevant provisions:

Article 44 — State shall endeavour to secure a Uniform Civil Code.
Article 25 — Freedom of conscience and religion.
Article 26 — Freedom to manage religious affairs.
Article 14 — Equality before law.
Article 15 — Prohibition of discrimination.
Concurrent List contains subjects such as marriage, divorce, adoption and succession.
