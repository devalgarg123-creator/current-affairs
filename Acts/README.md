2) Citizenship Act, 1955: core legislation that governs the acquisition, termination, and deprivation of Indian citizenship.

Ways to Acquire Citizenship

The act provides five primary methods to obtain Indian citizenship:
• Birth: Based on the date of birth in India and the citizenship status of the parents.
• Descent: For individuals born outside India whose parent or parents were Indian citizens.
• Registration: For persons of Indian origin, certain spouses of citizens, and minor children.
• Naturalization:For foreigners who meet specific residency duration and qualification requirements.
• Incorporation of Territory: If new territory becomes part of India, the government specifies the people who become citizens.

Loss of Citizenship
Indian citizenship can be lost through three mechanisms:
• Renunciation: A voluntary declaration to give up citizenship.
• Termination: Automatic loss upon voluntarily acquiring the citizenship of another country.
• Deprivation: Compulsory removal by the central government due to fraud, disloyalty, or prolonged absence.
